AI data use disclosure

Before you use any AI-assisted import, review how ApplyKit handles the content you share. You can also continue with manual entry at any time.

What is transmitted

When you use an AI-assisted import, ApplyKit transmits only the bounded extracted text from your source. The text may include sensitive information such as career, contact, or salary details. ApplyKit never sends your original document.

Purpose and providers

The transmitted text is processed solely to extract facts for your review into a Candidate Profile or Job Application draft. Processing uses a commercial AI provider category routed through OpenRouter. The current provider list is available from OpenRouter's model list, and its privacy notice governs how that provider handles transmitted text under a zero data retention agreement. The transmitted text is not used for training provider models.

Errors and auditing

Extraction is automated, so automated errors are possible and every imported value stays editable before you save. ApplyKit records only a content-free audit of each AI operation — it never retains your source text, prompts, or raw AI output.

Your control

You can withdraw consent at any time from your Account controls, which blocks future AI transmissions without deleting any data you already accepted. Accepted Candidate Profile and Job Application data stays under your ordinary editing and deletion controls.
